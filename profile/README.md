# v2.io

*Vis Veritatis*

v2-io is currently the umbrella for independent research and infrastructure under
[Joseph Wecker](https://github.com/josephwecker). Active work focuses on
architectural and empirical methodology for aligned language-model-based
agents.

- **[agentic-systems](https://github.com/v2-io/agentic-systems)** — formal
  architectural framework (Adaptation and Actuation Dynamics).
- **[hedge-embeddings](https://github.com/v2-io/hedge-embeddings)** —
  empirical research on language-model representation geometry.
- **[archema](https://github.com/v2-io/archema)** — declarative
  resource-oriented domain modeling (Ruby).
- **[autopax](https://github.com/v2-io/autopax)** — production-quality
  agent-harness substrate (BLAKE3 hash-chained audit logs, self-authored
  system-prompt loading, session-continuation discipline).
- **[udon](https://github.com/v2-io/udon)** /
  **[libudon](https://github.com/v2-io/libudon)** — markup notation designed
  for unambiguous reading and writing by language-model agents.

Repositories are public but have not yet been announced or circulated — by
design, while the formal results consolidated. Orienting to academic
engagement and peer review now.

At the moment, I haven't put anything up in the actual v2.io site. It is currently strictly a personal research umbrella.
